About ZenPetals

Who we are and what we believe in.

Our Story

ZenPetals opened in Bristol in 2018. We started with one simple idea: a spa where everyone feels welcome and no one feels rushed.

We believe that relaxation should be easy to access. Our three values are calm, inclusivity, and care. We apply these values to every treatment, every booking, and every conversation.

Over the years, we have learned that many of our clients prefer a quiet, predictable visit with no surprises. So we designed our space and our website around that preference. We are still learning, and we always welcome feedback.

Our Commitment to Accessibility

ZenPetals is designed to be welcoming for autistic visitors, people with sensory sensitivities, and anyone who prefers a calm, predictable experience.

Our website has no pop-ups, no flashing images, and no content that changes without you asking it to. You can increase the text size, switch to dark mode, and turn off all animations using the toolbar at the top of every page.

In our spa, you can ask your therapist to stop or change the treatment at any time. We use fragrance-free products unless you choose otherwise. Our rooms are softly lit and quiet.

If you have questions before you book, please email us at hello@example.com. We are happy to answer any question, no matter how small.
