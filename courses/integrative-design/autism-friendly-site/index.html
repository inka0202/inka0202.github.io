What We Offer

We offer three treatments. Each one is calm, quiet, and designed around you.

  • 🤲 Massage Therapy

    Massage Therapy

    A gentle, full-body massage to ease tension and help you rest. Our therapists work slowly and always check in with you first. You can ask them to avoid any area at any time.

    From £55 · 60 minutes

  • 🌿 Aromatherapy

    Aromatherapy

    A light massage using natural plant oils chosen for calm and comfort. You can choose your scent from a small, clearly labelled collection. Unscented oil is always available on request.

    From £50 · 50 minutes

  • ✨ Relaxation Facial

    Relaxation Facial

    A quiet, gentle facial treatment to soothe and hydrate your skin. We use fragrance-free products by default. The room is softly lit and quiet throughout.

    From £45 · 45 minutes
